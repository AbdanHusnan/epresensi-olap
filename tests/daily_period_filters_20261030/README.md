# Daily and period filters — 30 October 2026

The website now separates two independent date scopes:

1. **Daily attendance:** one reporting date and a department-name picker for
   daily KPI cards, department rankings, department summary and employee tables.
   The token endpoint rejects multi-date ranges for daily cards and tables.
2. **Attendance over time:** its own start/end dates and department-name picker
   for the daily attendance line chart and period availability status. Changing
   this section does not reload or aggregate the daily KPI cards.

The test-run selector applies to both sections and resets their defaults. Daily
views default to the run's first date; the trend defaults to its available test
period. Table text search continues to affect only daily detail tables.

Department suggestions search names from each run's reporting dimensions.
Names are loaded through an authenticated endpoint; managers can retrieve only
their assigned department. IDs appear as secondary text to distinguish identical
names. Selecting a name signs the department's ID internally. Department options
are regenerated by `deploy/hybrid/dashboards/configure_test_runs.py`.

No test attendance was loaded or changed. The gradual simulation remains paused
after batch 1: 30 October morning check-ins, zero checkouts. The 31 October date
remains unloaded; the trend does not invent zero attendance for it.
